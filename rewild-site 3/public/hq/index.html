Reviews

Customers get one email 14 days after you mark their order shipped (18 days after the order if it never was). Every review gets a one-time 15% thank-you code. Nothing shows on the website until you approve it, and only if the customer said we could share it. Trim anything that reads like a health claim before approving (use "..." where you cut).

Refer a friend

Give $20, get $20 · rewildmushrooms.com/refer/

Every customer gets a friend code (like JADE-7K3Q) on their thank-you page and in the review and reorder emails. Friends get $20 off a first order of $75+. 21 days after the friend's order (if it isn't refunded), the website emails the customer a one-time $20 REWARD code. Orders shipping to the customer's own address wait here for you.
